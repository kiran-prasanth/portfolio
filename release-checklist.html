Release Readiness Checklist

Pre-release gate used to coordinate sign-off from engineering, QA, and design before any client-facing release. Every item must be confirmed before a release is approved. One unchecked item is enough to hold the release.

Design and QA Sign-Off
All screens signed off by client against acceptance criteria defined at kickoff.Sign-off documented in email or Notion. Verbal confirmation alone does not count.
PM
QA sign-off complete on all screens included in this release.QA engineer confirms all test cases passed. Failed cases documented with disposition (fixed or accepted risk).
QA Lead
All screens verified at mobile viewport (360px and above).Mobile QA completed as part of standard QA cycle, not as a final afterthought.
QA Lead
Design-to-dev handoff package complete and confirmed by dev lead.Annotated Figma, component specs, interaction notes, and edge case documentation in place.
Design Lead
All open RAID items reviewed. No unresolved blockers.RAID log reviewed by PM. Any open items formally accepted or escalated before release proceeds.
PM
Engineering and Deployment
Release branch confirmed and reviewed by dev lead.No uncommitted changes in the release branch. Merge confirmed from staging.
Dev Lead
Staging environment matches production config.Environment-specific variables verified. No hardcoded staging values in release build.
Dev Lead
Rollback plan confirmed and documented before deployment begins.Rollback steps written out. Dev lead and PM both understand the trigger conditions.
Dev Lead / PM
Third-party integrations tested end-to-end in staging.Not applicable if no integrations in this release scope.
Dev Lead
Communications
Client informed of release date and expected downtime (if any) at least 48 hours before.Release communication sent by PM. Client confirms receipt.
PM
Internal teams (Sales, Customer Success where applicable) briefed on what is shipping.Release summary document shared. Covers what is in, what moved out, and why.
PM
Release record updated with what shipped, what moved out, and reasons for any changes.Maintained in Notion. Permanent record for audit and client reference.
PM
Rollback Conditions
Critical bug post-deploy
Dev lead initiates rollback immediately. PM informs client within 30 minutes with a status note and ETA for fix. No waiting for the next standup.
Integration failure
Third-party integration disabled at feature flag level. Rest of release stays live. Fix tracked as a hotfix sprint item with 24-hour resolution target.
Client requests halt
Rollback initiated within 1 hour of client request. Root cause documented before re-release. PM leads post-mortem within 48 hours.
