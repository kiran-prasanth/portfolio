Live RAID Log — Sample

Format used to track Risks, Issues, Assumptions, and Dependencies across 12-15 concurrent client projects. Each item is owned, dated, and reviewed weekly with department heads. Blockers are escalated before they impact timelines, not during standups.

All risks reviewed weekly. High priority items escalated to department head if mitigation is not actioned within 48 hours of logging.

Issues are logged the day they surface, not at the next standup. Each issue has a resolution plan within 24 hours.
