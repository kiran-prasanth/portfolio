End-to-End Delivery Workflow SOP

Version2.1
OwnerKiran Prasanth R
AdoptedFeb 2025
Applies ToAll client engagements
Purpose

This SOP defines the standard delivery process for all client projects at Brandshark, from project kickoff through final sign-off and handoff. It exists to ensure consistency, reduce mid-project misalignment, and give every team member a clear reference for what good delivery looks like.

This is not a bureaucratic checklist. Every step is here because the absence of it caused a real problem in a real project.

Delivery Phases
01 Project Kickoff Day 1
  • Scope document shared with client before kickoff. No kickoff call without a confirmed scope summary. Client to confirm understanding in writing before work begins.
  • Milestones and delivery dates confirmed. Sprint structure agreed at kickoff. Client review checkpoints added to shared calendar before Day 2.
  • Acceptance criteria defined for all Sprint 1 deliverables. PM and client agree on what done looks like before a single screen is designed.
  • Communication norms agreed. Review turnaround time, escalation path, and point of contact confirmed and documented.
  • RAID log opened. Assumptions captured on Day 1. First risks logged before Sprint 1 begins.
02 Sprint Planning Start of each sprint
  • Only spec-complete tickets enter the sprint. No ticket is pulled in unless the spec has been reviewed and confirmed by the relevant lead.
  • Every ticket has an owner and an estimate before sprint start. No unassigned or unestimated tickets in the sprint board.
  • Dependencies are mapped before planning closes. Anything that blocks a ticket is logged in the RAID log at this stage.
  • Capacity checked against team availability. No sprint is planned at 100% capacity. 10-15% buffer retained for unplanned blockers.
03 Sprint Execution and Daily Tracking Daily
  • PM reviews all open tickets daily. Tickets that have not moved in 48 hours are flagged. Owner is contacted same day.
  • Blockers are logged in RAID the day they surface. Not at the next standup. Not when they become critical.
  • Scope change requests are logged immediately. No scope change is absorbed verbally. Change request form is completed before any work on the change begins.
  • Board hygiene maintained throughout sprint. Tickets updated daily. No stale "in progress" items.
04 Client Review Gates Mid and end of sprint
  • Review materials sent 24 hours before the session. No reviewing materials during the call. Client reviews async, session is for decisions and sign-off.
  • Review sessions are time-boxed. Time limit set in the invite. Facilitator owns the clock.
  • Sign-off is written. Email, Slack, or document confirmation. Verbal approval alone does not count.
  • Revision scope is assessed before work begins. If revisions exceed the agreed revision allowance, a change request is raised before any work starts.
05 Final Delivery and Sign-Off Project close
  • Final deliverables reviewed against original acceptance criteria. Every AC is checked before the delivery package is assembled.
  • Handoff package includes all required assets. Files, documentation, spec links, and any developer notes as applicable.
  • Client sign-off received in writing before the project is marked closed.
  • RAID log closed. All open items resolved or formally accepted as post-project risks before closure.
  • Retrospective completed within one week of delivery. Outputs documented and action items assigned before the next sprint begins.
Escalation Triggers
Client review delayed > 48 hrs
PM follows up with client point of contact. If unresolved by Hour 72, escalated to account lead with timeline impact note.
Blocker unresolved > 24 hrs
PM escalates to department head with mitigation options. No blocker sits unresolved for 48 hours without leadership visibility.
Scope change > 2 days effort
Requires PM and department head sign-off before entering the delivery plan. Client informed of impact before work begins.
Sprint close rate < 80%
Root cause documented in sprint retrospective. Action item raised and owned. If it happens two sprints in a row, resourcing reviewed with leadership.
